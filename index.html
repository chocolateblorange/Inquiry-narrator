ACCESSI(RATE)

Accessible, authentic narration of complex documents. Designed to help neurodivergent people and people with disabilities access the same quality of information as everyone else, in alternative formats that work for them. Our goal is narration that respects the meaning, context and emotional weight of the written word.

1. Upload your PDF

Up to 100 MB. Text-based PDFs only in this MVP.

2. Listen

Automatic mode uses restrained, contextual delivery. Your device's speech engine determines how expressive the result can be.

Device voices can only approximate emotional delivery. An optional expressive speech engine uses actual synthesis styles when configured. Azure F0 includes a limited free monthly character allowance; paid tiers may incur charges. Text sent for expressive speech is processed by Azure. Current MVP uses voices supplied by your browser or operating system without cloning any actor's voice. Text is processed in memory and not deliberately stored on the server. Browser speech support varies by device.
