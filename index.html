ACCESSI(RATE)

Accessible, authentic narration of complex documents. Designed to help neurodivergent people and people with disabilities access the same quality of information as everyone else, in alternative formats that work for them. Our goal is real, professionally licensed human voices for natural, trustworthy presentation.

1. Upload your PDF

Up to 100 MB. Text-based PDFs only in this MVP.

2. Listen

Default: measured documentary delivery for serious reports. Voice tone still depends on the voice installed on your device.

Natural pacing adds sentence-aware pauses and paragraph breaks, but voice expressiveness still depends on your device. Current MVP uses voices supplied by your browser or operating system while professional human-voice licensing is explored. No actor's voice is cloned. Text is processed in memory and not deliberately stored on the server. Browser speech support varies by device.
